ADDITIONAL NOTES
================================================================================

DESIGN DECISIONS:

1. Why fixed 110px display?
   - Maximum space for buttons while keeping display readable
   - 32px result font is clear and large enough
   - Expression at 14px prevents clutter
   - Total fits all phones without scrolling

2. Why NeverScrollableScrollPhysics?
   - GridView fits in remaining space after display
   - No need to scroll - forces conscious layout
   - Prevents accidental scroll
   - Cleaner user experience

3. Why ScaleTransition instead of other animations?
   - GPU-efficient (uses transform, not rebuilds)
   - Natural, tactile feel (0.95 scale is subtle)
   - 150ms duration feels responsive
   - Consistent with Material Design

4. Why multi-layer shadows?
   - Primary shadow: 4px offset for depth
   - Secondary shadow: 1px for edge definition
   - Creates 3D appearance without overdoing it
   - Professional and subtle

5. Why responsive 5-6 columns?
   - 5 columns: Optimal for phones (360-480dp)
   - 6 columns: Fits tablets better (600+dp)
   - Automatic via MediaQuery
   - No manual breakpoint management

6. Why 6px spacing instead of 8px?
   - Saves ~16px per row (4 gaps × 2px reduction)
   - Still visually clear and not cramped
   - Critical for fitting 8 rows without scroll
   - Standard Android spacing unit
